Short Description

Slide Cafe is a cozy word puzzle for iOS and Android, built around a distinctive sliding mechanic. Move letter columns up and down to form words across the center row, take on daily challenges, explore different game modes, and unlock new cafe themes as you play.

Long Description

Slide Cafe brings a different approach to the familiar word puzzle genre with a distinctive sliding mechanic. Instead of connecting letters or filling a traditional grid, players move letter columns up and down to form the correct word across the center row.

Each game mode offers a different way to play. Relax Puzzle provides clue-based puzzles without pressure, while Daily Challenge introduces a new limited-move puzzle each day. Word Hunt challenges players to uncover five hidden words, with every additional valid word earning extra bonus rewards. As players progress, new Word Packs introduce increasingly challenging puzzles and a broader range of words.

With clean visuals, fluid interactions, unlockable cafe themes, leaderboards, and leagues, Slide Cafe combines a calm atmosphere with light competition. It is easy to pick up, satisfying to solve, and rewarding to return to each day.
