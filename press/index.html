Short Description

Slide Cafe is a cozy word puzzle game for iOS and Android. Slide letters into words in a warm cafe-inspired world, with daily challenges, relaxing clue puzzles, and free-form word hunts — calm to play, satisfying to master.

Long Description

Slide Cafe blends a refined cafe mood with fast word puzzles. A smooth, intuitive word-sliding mechanic sits at the center of the game: clean visuals, fluid motion, and daily challenges create a calm but competitive rhythm that rewards repeat play.

Daily Challenge offers one focused puzzle every day, Relax Puzzle delivers clue-guided, no-pressure solving, Word Hunt lets you swipe freely for words, and themed Word Packs keep the content fresh. Leaderboards and leagues add a light competitive layer, while cloud save keeps progress safe without requiring an account. Slide Cafe is free to play; a one-time Slide Cafe Pro purchase removes interstitial ads.
