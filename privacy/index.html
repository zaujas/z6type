Privacy Policy for Slide Cafe – Word Game

Effective Date: April 23, 2026

Last Updated: July 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application Slide Cafe – Word Game ("Slide Cafe", the "App", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the App on iOS or Android devices.

Slide Cafe is published under the z6type brand by an independent developer. z6type is not an incorporated legal entity. References to "we" in this Policy mean the developer operating under the z6type brand.

By downloading, installing, or using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Scope and Summary

Slide Cafe is a word puzzle game designed to work without asking you to create a traditional account or provide direct contact information.

  • The App does not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, or login credentials.
  • The App automatically creates a pseudonymous Supabase user ID so that online features, leaderboards, cloud saves, and purchase verification can work.
  • Your chosen in-game nickname, avatar, gameplay progress, virtual-currency balances, and related game data may be stored locally and synced to Supabase.
  • RevenueCat processes store purchase history and entitlement information so that Slide Cafe Pro and other eligible purchases can be validated and restored.
  • Google Firebase Analytics measures product usage, and Firebase Crashlytics processes crash and diagnostic information.
  • Google AdMob processes advertising and ad-interaction data. Advertising identifiers are used only when available and permitted by applicable consent and platform settings.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play process payments. We do not receive or store your payment-card details.

2. Who We Are (Data Controller)

  • Brand: z6type
  • Role: Independent developer (not an incorporated company)
  • Contact email: info@z6type.com

Depending on the service and applicable law, Apple, Google, Supabase, and RevenueCat may act as our processors/service providers or as independent controllers for information they process under their own terms and privacy policies.

3. Information We Do Not Request

  • We do not ask you to provide your real name, personal email address, phone number, postal address, or profile photo through the App.
  • We do not access your contacts, calendar, SMS, call logs, camera, microphone, photos, or precise GPS location.
  • We do not receive your payment-card number, billing address, or app-store password.
  • We do not sell personal information for money.

4. Information We and Our Service Providers Process

4.1 Information stored on your device

The App stores information locally, including:

  • Game state, unfinished puzzle data, completed levels, scores, hints used, and move counts
  • Settings such as language, sound, haptics, tutorial state, and onboarding flags
  • Virtual-currency and inventory balances
  • A locally cached copy of purchase-entitlement status so previously validated purchase benefits can remain recognized when the network or verification service is temporarily unavailable. This cache does not control or restrict offline gameplay, which is available regardless of Slide Cafe Pro status.
  • Locally generated session identifiers and player seed values
  • Authentication session material used to resume the App's pseudonymous Supabase user

Some local data may also be included in operating-system backup or cloud-storage features that you have enabled. On iOS, secure or iCloud-backed recovery data may persist after reinstall. On Android, uninstalling the App will usually remove locally stored data unless the operating system restores a backup.

4.2 Pseudonymous Supabase identity, profile, leaderboard, and cloud save

The App automatically creates a Supabase user record without asking you to enter a name, email address, or password. Supabase assigns a unique pseudonymous user ID (UUID) and issues session tokens. This is an authenticated technical identity used only for the App's online features.

Information associated with this user ID may include:

  • Your chosen in-game nickname and avatar
  • Leaderboard and league data, such as scores, time, moves, hints used, locale, game mode, league tier, points, and badges
  • Cloud-save data, including progress, unfinished games, settings, streaks, inventory, virtual currencies, and purchase-related game state
  • Player-safety preferences, including the pseudonymous IDs of players you choose to hide
  • Username-moderation reports, including the reporter and reported pseudonymous IDs, reported nickname, league context, reason, status, and timestamps
  • Timestamps, schema versions, and technical records needed to synchronize and protect the data

On iOS, the App may use an app-scoped iCloud/CloudKit user record identifier and iCloud-stored recovery material to reconnect the same pseudonymous Supabase identity after reinstall or on another eligible device. The App may derive internal, non-user-facing recovery credentials from that app-scoped identifier. This is not Sign in with Apple: we do not receive your Apple ID email address, Apple password, or Apple profile information.

Purpose / legal basis: app functionality, cloud save, leaderboards, fraud and abuse prevention, purchase verification, support, and our legitimate interests and/or performance of the service requested by you.

4.3 RevenueCat purchases and entitlements

We use RevenueCat to retrieve product information, process purchase results from Apple or Google, validate purchases, restore eligible purchases, and determine whether Slide Cafe Pro or another entitlement is active.

RevenueCat may process:

  • The same pseudonymous Supabase user ID as the RevenueCat App User ID
  • Product identifiers, offering and price information, transaction and purchase history, entitlement status, purchase and expiration dates, and restore status
  • Apple receipt information or Google Play purchase tokens supplied by the store
  • App version, platform, device type, operating-system version, locale, country/currency information, IP address, and SDK-related technical events

The App also sends an authenticated request to a Supabase Edge Function, which queries RevenueCat to verify Slide Cafe Pro status. RevenueCat and our backend do not receive your payment-card details from this process.

Purpose / legal basis: purchase fulfillment, entitlement management, fraud prevention, customer support, analytics, and performance of the purchase contract.

4.4 Google Firebase Analytics

Firebase Analytics may process:

  • A Firebase app instance or installation identifier
  • Device model, operating-system version, locale, app version, and general technical information
  • Install source, first-open time, session information, and coarse location inferred from IP address
  • Gameplay and product-interaction events, such as game progress, feature use, store/paywall actions, purchase-result events, and advertising events

We do not intentionally send your real name, personal email address, phone number, or the words you enter during gameplay to Firebase Analytics. Analytics event names and parameters are limited to the technical and gameplay information needed to understand feature performance.

Purpose / legal basis: analytics, game balancing, product improvement, and measuring feature usage, based on legitimate interests and/or consent where required.

4.5 Firebase Crashlytics and diagnostics

Firebase Crashlytics may process crash stack traces, error messages, diagnostic breadcrumbs, app and operating-system versions, device model and state, timestamps, Firebase/Crashlytics installation identifiers, and technical attributes added to diagnose a failure. Diagnostic records are used to identify affected installations and troubleshoot stability issues. We do not intentionally attach your real name or personal contact information to crash reports.

Purpose / legal basis: diagnosing crashes, maintaining security and reliability, and improving App performance, based on legitimate interests.

4.6 Google AdMob

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may process:

  • Advertising identifiers, such as Apple's IDFA or Android advertising ID, when available
  • IP address and coarse location inferred from IP
  • Device, operating-system, language, app, and network information
  • Ad requests, impressions, clicks, exposure, closures, rewarded-ad completion, and fraud-prevention signals

On iOS, the App requests App Tracking Transparency permission before using data for tracking. If permission is denied, the IDFA is unavailable to the App and ads are requested without that identifier. In the EEA, UK, Switzerland, and other regions where required, consent choices are requested before personalized advertising or non-essential storage is used.

Purpose / legal basis: displaying and measuring ads, rewarding optional ad views, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and personalized advertising where valid consent has been provided.

4.7 Apple App Store and Google Play purchases

Apple App Store or Google Play processes the payment transaction and provides the App and RevenueCat with the store data needed to fulfill, validate, and restore a purchase. We do not receive your full payment-card information, store password, or billing address. Refunds and payment disputes are handled by the store under its own policies.

4.8 Local notifications and reminders

With your permission, the App may schedule notifications on your device for daily challenges, streaks, league events, and free rewards. These reminders are scheduled locally by the App using game state and times already stored on your device. Slide Cafe does not obtain or upload a remote push-notification token for these reminders.

You can deny notification permission, disable notifications later in your device settings, or continue using the App without enabling them.

Purpose / legal basis: optional gameplay reminders and app functionality, based on your permission.

We disclose information only as needed:

  • Between the App, our Supabase backend, RevenueCat, and the applicable app store to validate and restore purchases

8. Data Retention

  • Local progress and settings remain until you clear App data, uninstall the App, or the operating system removes them. Eligible iCloud or platform backups may remain under your platform settings.
  • Supabase user, leaderboard, league, cloud-save, player-hiding, and moderation-report records are retained while needed to provide the features, protect the service, investigate abuse, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. You may request deletion as described below.
  • RevenueCat retains purchase and entitlement records as needed to validate and restore purchases, provide analytics/support, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations and its privacy policy.
  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics data follow the retention settings and schedules described by Google Firebase.
  • AdMob data follows Google's advertising retention policies and your consent/settings.
  • Apple and Google retain store transaction records under their own legal obligations and policies.

Deletion from our systems may not delete records that Apple, Google, or another independent controller must retain, and backup copies may take additional time to expire.

If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal information in violation of this section, contact info@z6type.com.

10. Your Privacy Rights and Data Deletion

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or receive a copy of personal data, object to certain processing, withdraw consent, and complain to a supervisory authority.

You can permanently delete the App's pseudonymous account from Settings > Delete Account. This removes the Supabase Auth user and associated profile, leaderboard, league, player-hiding, moderation-report, entitlement, and cloud-save records; removes the related RevenueCat customer record under our control; deletes Slide Cafe's private CloudKit game-save record on iOS when available; clears local gameplay data and account-recovery material; and returns the App to its first-run onboarding.

App Store or Google Play transaction records controlled by Apple or Google are not deleted by this action, and deleting the account does not cancel a store subscription. Eligible purchases can be restored later through the applicable store. If in-App deletion is unavailable or you need help with another privacy request, email info@z6type.com. Because we do not request direct contact identifiers in the App, we may ask for your in-game nickname, pseudonymous user ID, transaction identifier, or other technical information needed to locate and verify the relevant records.

You can also:

  • Reset or limit advertising identifiers through your device settings.
  • Deny or change iOS App Tracking Transparency permission in iOS Settings.
  • Change consent choices through any privacy controls made available in the App or by the advertising provider.
  • Request purchase refunds, cancel subscriptions, or manage store transaction history through Apple or Google.
