Privacy Policy for Slide Cafe – Word Game

Effective Date: April 23, 2026

Last Updated: April 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application Slide Cafe – Word Game ("Slide Cafe", the "App", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the App on iOS or Android devices.

Slide Cafe is published under the z6type brand, operated by an individual independent developer (the "Developer"). z6type is not an incorporated legal entity. References to "we" in this Policy mean the Developer operating under the z6type brand.

By downloading, installing, or using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Scope and Summary

Slide Cafe is a single-player word puzzle game designed to operate with as little personal information as possible.

  • No account, sign-up, sign-in, or profile is required.
  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, address, or photo.
  • We do not collect posts, uploads, or other user-generated content.
  • Game progress, settings, and entitlements are stored locally on your device using secure platform storage.
  • We use Google Firebase Analytics for analytics and Google AdMob for advertising.
  • In-app purchases are processed only by Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive or store payment card details.

2. Who We Are (Data Controller)

  • Brand: z6type
  • Role: Independent developer (not an incorporated company)
  • Contact email: info@z6type.com

Third-party providers such as Google, Apple, and Google Play act as independent controllers or joint controllers for the information they process under their own policies.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect direct identifiers such as real name, email, or address.
  • We do not require social login or account creation.
  • We do not access contacts, calendar, SMS, call logs, camera, microphone, photos, or precise GPS.
  • We do not upload your saved progress or device settings to our own servers.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not use personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising without consent.

4. Information We (and Our SDKs) Do Process

4.1 Information stored only on your device

  • Game state, unfinished puzzle data, hints used, and move counts
  • Settings such as language, sound, haptics, tutorial state, and onboarding flags
  • Entitlements such as ad-free, premium access, and expiry timestamps
  • Limited local analytics history for diagnostics
  • Locally generated session identifiers and random player seed values

This data remains on your device unless you explicitly share it. It is deleted when you uninstall the App.

4.2 Google Firebase Analytics

Firebase Analytics may process a Firebase app instance ID, device model, operating system version, locale, app version, install source, first open timestamp, session data, coarse location inferred from IP, gameplay events, and crash or error diagnostics.

We do not send direct identifiers such as name or email to Firebase. Event parameters are sanitized and truncated before logging.

Purpose / Legal basis: analytics, bug fixing, balancing gameplay, and measuring feature usage, based on legitimate interests and/or consent where required.

4.3 Google AdMob

AdMob may process advertising identifiers (IDFA or Android Advertising ID, where available), coarse location inferred from IP, device and language data, app identifier, and ad interaction data such as impressions, clicks, closures, and rewarded ad completion.

  • On iOS, declining App Tracking Transparency results in non-personalized ads.
  • In the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, consent is collected before personalized ads or non-essential identifiers are used where required.
  • Users can revisit ad choices from Settings -> Privacy.

Purpose / Legal basis: monetization, contextual ads, frequency capping, and personalized advertising where consent is provided.

4.4 In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play Billing. We only receive the receipt or product identifier needed to validate and unlock the purchase on your device. We do not receive your payment card, billing address, Apple ID, or Google account email.

4.5 OS-level crash and diagnostics

Apple and Google may collect crash logs and performance data under your operating system privacy settings. Those flows are governed by Apple and Google, not by z6type.

6. How We Use the Information

  1. To provide gameplay and save progress locally on your device.
  2. To unlock and restore purchases and entitlements.
  3. To measure aggregate usage, improve balancing, and fix crashes.
  4. To serve ads and limit ad repetition.
  5. To comply with platform policies, law, and our Terms of Service.

7. How We Share Information

  • With the third parties listed above for analytics, ads, payments, and update delivery.
  • With Apple and Google as required for store distribution and billing.
  • To comply with law, legal process, safety, fraud prevention, or rights protection.
  • In connection with a business transfer involving the App or z6type assets.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising except through AdMob as described above and subject to applicable consent rules.

8. Data Retention

  • On-device data remains until you delete app data or uninstall the App.
  • Firebase retention follows the project's configured retention settings.
  • Ad data retention follows Google AdMob's own policies.
  • Purchase receipts are retained by Apple or Google under their own policies.

9. Children's Privacy

Slide Cafe is a general-audience word puzzle game, but it is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK where applicable), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal information in violation of this section, contact info@z6type.com.

10. Rights in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland

  • Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and portability rights
  • Right to withdraw consent
  • Right to complain to a supervisory authority

Because we do not collect direct identifiers, additional technical details may be required to associate a request with a specific device or analytics record.

11. Rights in California (CCPA / CPRA)

  • Right to know, access, delete, and correct personal information
  • Right to opt out of "sale" or "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights

We do not sell personal information for money. Personalized advertising may constitute "sharing" under CPRA, and you can opt out via platform settings or in-app privacy controls where available.

12. Other Jurisdictions

Users in jurisdictions such as Brazil, Canada, Australia, Japan, South Korea, and Turkey may have similar privacy rights under local law. Contact us at info@z6type.com to exercise those rights.

13. Security

We rely on platform-level security, code signing, app sandboxing, encrypted transport used by SDK providers, and the fact that we do not operate a backend storing per-user profiles. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

14. Permissions Requested by the App

  • App Tracking Transparency on iOS for personalized ads
  • Internet access for ads and analytics
  • In-app billing for purchases
  • Haptics / vibration for gameplay feedback
  • Audio playback for sound effects

The App does not request camera, microphone, photos, contacts, calendar, precise location, Bluetooth scanning, SMS, or call logs.

15. Platform Store Disclosures

  • Data linked to you: none maintained by us
  • Data not linked to you: usage data, diagnostics, identifiers, coarse location, and platform-level purchase history
  • Tracking data is used only if you grant ATT permission for personalized ads
