Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data Slide Cafe may process, how that information may be used, and which rights users may exercise.

1. Data we collect

  • The app follows a minimum data collection approach and only collects data required to operate, maintain, and improve the experience.
  • Device type, operating system version, basic crash logs, performance metrics, and in-game score or progress data may be processed.
  • Features such as leaderboards or support requests may store a nickname or email address provided directly by the user.
  • Slide Cafe is not intended to collect sensitive personal data unless that becomes necessary under applicable law and with the required permissions or disclosures.

2. Why we use data

  • To run the game, debug issues, and monitor performance.
  • To support leaderboards, daily challenges, and product improvements.
  • To respond to support requests and prevent abuse or fraud.

3. Third-party services and advertising

  • The app may use third-party services such as Google AdMob for advertising, analytics, or measurement.
  • These services may process device identifiers, advertising IDs, approximate location, or usage data according to their own privacy policies.
  • Where legally required, the app may present consent or permission flows for personalized advertising and related data processing.
  • z6type expects any partner receiving data to maintain privacy and security protections aligned with this policy.

4. Retention, security, and deletion

  • Data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary, taking into account legal obligations, fraud prevention, and support workflows.
  • Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards may include encrypted transport, access restrictions, and platform-level protections.
  • Users may remove local data by uninstalling the app, and server-side or support-related deletion requests may be submitted by email.

5. User rights

  • Depending on applicable law, users may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis.
  • Requests may be sent to support@z6type.com.
  • Additional identity verification or retention obligations may apply where required by law.

6. Children and updates

  • The app is intended for a general audience and is not designed to intentionally collect personal data from children.
  • If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page and additional in-app notice may be provided when appropriate.
